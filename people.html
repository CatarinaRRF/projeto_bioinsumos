MSF Group coordinator
Group coordinator

Maria Sueli Soares Felipe

Biochemist, molecular biologist, and professor-researcher at Universidade Católica de Brasília, bringing decades of experience in genomics, biotechnology, and collaborative research.

Maria Sueli Soares Felipe earned her degree in Chemistry from the University of Brasília, a master's degree in Molecular Biology from UnB, and a doctorate in Biochemistry from the University of São Paulo, including a research period at the University of Manchester. She was a professor at UnB from 1980 to 2010 and was named Professor Emerita in 2019. She has been a senior collaborating researcher at UnB since 2010 and a professor-researcher at UCB.

Her career includes leading and contributing to national and international genomics networks, coordinating funded research and biotechnology programs, and serving in scientific leadership and policy roles, including the presidency of Brazil's National Biosafety Technical Commission (CTNBio). Her research experience spans molecular biology, host-pathogen interactions, fungal and microbial genomics, nanobiotechnology, and the development of biotechnology applications.
